  1. Introduction                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  PackPact ("we", "our", or "us") is a trip packing organizer app that helps groups plan and manage their packing lists. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and
  your rights regarding your data.                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                            
  By using PackPact, you agree to this Privacy Policy.                                                                                                                                                   
                                                            
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  2. Information We Collect                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  a) Account Information
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  When you sign up, we collect:                                                                                                                                                                          
  - Full name                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Email address                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Profile photo URL (if you sign in with Google)          
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  b) Trip & Packing Data                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  When you use the app, we store:                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Trip names, destinations, and travel dates                                                                                                                                                           
  - Packing list items you create or are assigned to you                                                                                                                                                 
  - Trip invite codes                                       
  - Chat messages within trips                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Documents and links you add to trips                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  c) Device & Usage Information                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token — to send you push notifications                                                                                                                                
  - Device type and OS version (collected automatically by Firebase)                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  d) Information You Do NOT Need to Provide                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - We do not collect payment information                                                                                                                                                                
  - We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location

  4. Third-Party Services                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  PackPact uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Google Firebase Authentication — account sign-in and security                                                                                                                                        
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy                                                                                                                                                            
  - Google Firestore — cloud storage for your trip and packing data                                                                                                                                      
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy                                                                                                                                                            
  - Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications                                                                                                                                                        
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy                                                                                                                                                            
  - Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method                                                                                                                                                             
  https://policies.google.com/privacy                                                                                                                                                                    
  - REST Countries API (restcountries.com) — destination country information lookup. No personal data is sent to this service.                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  5. Data Storage and Security                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - All your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore) in Google's cloud infrastructure.
  - Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.                                                                                                                                            
  - Access to trip data is restricted so that only members of a trip can read or write to it.                                                                                                            
  - We implement reasonable technical safeguards, but no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password.                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  6. Data Retention                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Your account and trip data is retained as long as your account is active.
  - If you delete your account, your personal data (name, email) will be deleted within 30 days.                                                                                                         
  - Trip data you created may remain visible to other trip members until they also delete it.

  7. Your Rights                                                                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Access the personal data we hold about you                                                                                                                                                           
  - Correct inaccurate data                                                                                                                                                                              
  - Delete your account and associated data                                                                                                                                                              
  - Export your data                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  8. Children's Privacy                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  PackPact is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information,
  please contact us and we will delete it.                                                                                                                                                               
                                                            
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  9. Push Notifications                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  We send push notifications for:
  - Trip reminders (7 days, 3 days, 1 day before your trip)                                                                                                                                              
  - Nudges from trip members to pack items                                                                                                                                                               
  - In-app chat messages when the app is in the background                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings → Notifications → PackPact.
