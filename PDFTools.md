Privacy Policy for Offline PDF Tools

Last Updated: March 8, 2024

Offline PDF Tools ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. 
This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. 100% Offline Processing

   Offline PDF Tools is designed to work entirely offline.
   All document processing—including scanning, merging,
   splitting, converting (Word/Image to PDF), and compressing—happens locally on your mobile device.
   We do not upload your documents, images, or any personal data to our servers or any third-party servers.

2. Information Collection and Use
   We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data.
   Document Data: Any documents or images you select or capture within the app are processed in temporary memory or saved directly to your device's local storage.
   We have no access to these files.

   Camera Permission: Used solely for the "Scan to PDF" feature to capture document images via the Google ML Kit Document Scanner.

   Storage Permission: Used to read files you select for processing and to save the final PDF results to your device's "Downloads" or "Documents" folder.

3. Third-Party Services
   The app utilizes Google Play Services for the "Scan to PDF" feature (Google ML Kit Document Scanner).
   Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage data as part of their service delivery.
   You can view their policy here: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy
   Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
   Since no data is transmitted from the device,
   we do not store or share any information belonging to users of any age.

5. Security
   The security of your documents relies on your device’s own security.
   Because we never transmit your data over the internet, your files are protected from interception by third parties during the processing phase.
   
7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
   We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time.
   Changes are effective immediately upon posting the updated policy.
